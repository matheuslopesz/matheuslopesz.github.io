2025 flew by for me. I barely blinked and it evaporated. This year I want
it to be different; I want to watch it go by slowly.
At the very end of last year I made a few new commitments,
and writing was one of them. Writing for the sake of writing, but also to make
peace with a skill that, in the age of AI, needs more and more
polishing. As a software engineer, I believe that writing and being
understood is as important as knowing the difference between backend and frontend
when you work on the web.

## Starting to make peace with writing
I loved reading when I was younger, but only fiction.
I lived the stories in my head, every written word, for hours.
But that was reading for fun. When it came to technical reading, especially from college onward, I started to prefer online video lessons.

Back when I was studying for the university entrance exams, a few YouTube channels showed me new ways to learn, and I went all in. College was no different, but I started to notice the difference in effort between understanding someone talking in a recorded lecture and reading the words at my own pace. My pace isn't 1.5x, nor 1.45x. It's simply my pace.

Over the years, I piled up some books, bought and inherited. That made it clear to me
that it wasn't as if I didn't know books existed or hadn't tried, but I just couldn't get into anything I set out to read.

When I decided to try again, with an open heart and understanding that this time would be different, I began to understand what reading meant to me.
Realizing that with a book I could set the exact pace at which I acquire knowledge was a turning point in my relationship with reading, and that's how this process of "making peace" began.

I'm at the very beginning of all this, but right away it became clear that I need a
little bit more: writing. Reading is the ability to understand concepts and ideas through symbols. Writing is the ability to describe concepts and ideas with symbols. By a very
small margin, we don't have identical definitions.

You'll be seeing more of me around here. I don't know exactly how much, but I'll certainly be here. Talking about technology and maybe a bit of magic. Happy New Year!!!
